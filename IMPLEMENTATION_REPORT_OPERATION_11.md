# Operation 11 — Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery 360

Implemented an end-to-end recovery-readiness operation after reliability/incident response.

- Added continuity plans with criticality, RTO/RPO, ownership, recovery procedure and review dates.
- Added auditable recovery drills and outcomes.
- Added recovery evidence checkpoints for backup, restore, dependencies, credentials, contacts and runbook reviews.
- Enforced RLS and RPC-only mutation boundaries.
- Reused existing staff RBAC permissions.
- Added Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery 360 admin console.
- Added cumulative CI verifier and migration manifest registration.
- Explicitly preserved the boundary that provider backups/restores are external evidence and are not fabricated by application records.
